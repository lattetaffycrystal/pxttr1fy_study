Study Land is a personal study space designed like a pastel browser window. It’s built for staying focused and making revision feel a little more fun. Upload your own photos for the collage, swap the sticker icons for your own, and choose a custom background and mouse cursor to match your style. The notes and goals tabs hold your to-do lists and study targets, and the diary lets you start a fresh page each day to reflect on how studying went. Everything on the page is editable. Click any text to rewrite it, and use “Save page as file” to keep a copy with all your changes built in.
